Nigerian Pof-Pof

Right, so: imagine a donut, but softer, bite-sized, and perfect when straight out of the frier.

That's Pof-Pof, a fried pastry made of flour, sugar, and yeast.

It's a bit of a process, but not difficult. Just use my handy-dandy instructions!
There is a waiting period of 1-2 hours! Keep that in mind. You can also raise the dough overnight to avoid the boring part.

Ingredients

For this delectable snack, we'll need:

  • Yeast
  • Flour
  • Warm Water
  • Sugar
  • Oil
  • Optional:Nutella
  • Optional:Icing Sugar

Method

  1. 1." Mix your salt, water, sugar, and yeast together. Set aside for 5 minutes. The warm water will activate the yeast"
  2. 2." Add the flour and mix. Now you have dough! Let this rise for 1-2 hours. Sorry."
  3. 3." Heat the oil, deep enough for a deep fry!"
  4. 4." Sprinkle some flour into the oil to test if it's hot enough. If the flour sizzles, we're ready to go!"
  5. 5." (CAREFULLY!) drop a tablespoon full of batter into the hot oil. Keep doing this until the sauce pan is 3/4 full."
  6. 6." Once the balls solidify & turn a little golden on the side in the oil, USE A SPOON(!!!!) to flip them over."
  7. 7." Once the other side is golden, take it out! And you're DONE!"
  8. 7." If you'd like, sprinkle icing sugar on all of them, and put aside a bowl of nutella to dip them in."

Job's a goodun. Your pof-pof is ready to eat, and is best served fresh from the frier. Blooming delicious, mate.
